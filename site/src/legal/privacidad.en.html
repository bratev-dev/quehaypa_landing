Privacy and personal data policy

Effective from: [PUBLICATION DATE] · Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]

This policy explains how we handle your personal data when you use the ¿Qué hay pa'? app (the "app") and this website. It follows Colombian Statutory Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015). The Spanish version prevails in case of discrepancy.

1. Data controller

  • Controller: Alta Kurtosis Software Solutions
  • Contact email: quehaypaa@gmail.com
  • Domicile: Colombia

2. What data we process

In the app

  • Account data: first name, last name, email and password. Passwords are stored as a hash, never in plain text.
  • Profile data: date of birth and profile photo.
  • Preferences and usage: interests, budget, saved plans, reminders, bookings, reviews and votes.
  • Device location: to show plans near you and place them on the map.
  • Real-time location with friends: so your friends in the app can see where you are. [Confirm: whether users turn it on and off, and exactly who can see it.]
  • Friends and notifications: friend requests, friend list and in-app notifications.
  • Activity log: searches and interactions in the app, used to improve recommendations and produce statistics for businesses.
  • Business and organizer data: ID or tax number (NIT), business name and contact phone, used to verify who publishes events.

On this website

The website does not ask for your data or use tracking cookies. It stores your language, light or dark mode and whether you paused animations in your own browser (local storage); that information is not sent to us. To work, the site is served from GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts and an animation library served by cdnjs (Cloudflare); like any website, those services receive technical connection data such as your IP address.

3. Why we use your data

  • To create and manage your account, let you sign in and recover your password.
  • To show you events, places and activities based on your interests, budget and location.
  • To save your plans and send you reminders and in-app notifications.
  • To enable social features: friends and location sharing with friends.
  • To verify businesses and organizers and show trustworthy events.
  • To give businesses and organizers statistics about their posts. [Confirm: that these statistics are aggregated and do not identify users.]
  • To keep the app secure, prevent fraud and handle your requests.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Consent

By creating your account and accepting this policy you authorize us to process your data for the purposes above. The app will also ask for permission to access your device location; you can refuse or withdraw it in your phone settings, although some features will stop working.

5. Who we share data with

  • Railway: hosts the app's servers and database, the authentication service and password-recovery emails.
  • Mapbox: displays maps; it receives the coordinates of the areas you view on the map.
  • Other users: what you post (reviews, votes, events) and the location you share with your friends.
  • Authorities: when required by law or court order.

These providers may have servers outside Colombia, so your data may be stored in other countries. [Confirm: Railway server region.]

6. Your rights

As the data subject you have the right to:

  • Know, update and correct your personal data.
  • Request proof of the consent you gave us.
  • Be informed about how we have used your data.
  • File complaints with Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC).
  • Revoke your consent and ask us to delete your data, unless we are legally required to keep it.
  • Access your personal data free of charge.

7. How to exercise your rights

  • In the app: you can edit your profile and delete your account.
  • By email: write to quehaypaa@gmail.com with your name, your account email and your request.

We answer inquiries within ten (10) business days and claims within fifteen (15) business days from the day after we receive them. If we cannot answer in time, we will tell you why and when we will, within the limits set by law.

8. Minors

The app is only for people 18 and older. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it and its data.

9. Security

We use technical measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and access control to our services. No system is infallible; if an incident affects your data, we will inform you and report it as required by law.

10. How long we keep your data

11. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will notify you in the app or on this website before they take effect, and ask for new consent when the law requires it.
